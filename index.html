Welcome to Dalla Al-Sahra

Services

We provide VIP wedding and hospitality service across the UAE. Our young men and women are trained to serve traditional Arabic items, booked by the day, week, month or year.

Traditional weddings

Warm Arabic welcome for the couple, families and guests, with an elegant setup from the first cup to the last.

Corporate and VIP

Discreet, polished service for offices, ceremonies, exhibitions and welcome receptions.

Majlis and home

Hosts who pour, present and attend to guests in the traditional way, at home or in the majlis.
